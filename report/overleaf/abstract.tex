This project investigates autonomous landing of four UAVs on assigned pads under wind disturbance and aerodynamic drag. A simulation combines gusty wind, delayed velocity commands, noisy observations and collision-aware landing rules for Tello and Matrice 30 (M30) profiles. Parameter-sharing reinforcement learning controls approach and descent, with MAPPO and PPO (IPPO) used to evaluate two training designs within the system. A GUI supports fleet telemetry and Tello Gazebo visualisation. Over 100 evaluation episodes per condition, the best evaluated policies achieved 91\% fleet success for Tello at 3 m/s and 45\% for M30 at 10 m/s, with no observed collisions. Strong-wind failures were mainly missed pads. The implementation is available at \url{https://github.com/haohao9555/tello-multi-landing}.
